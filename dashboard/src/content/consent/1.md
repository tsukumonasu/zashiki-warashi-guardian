# Face registration notice

Please read this before you add a photo. Only the person in the photo, or a parent or guardian, should register it.

The doll compares the faces of visitors with the photos you register, so she can greet family and be wary of strangers. For this, one photo is sent to your own AWS account. It is kept briefly in a staging area and then deleted. What stays is a numeric face vector in an Amazon Rekognition collection, together with the display name you type. The photo itself is not kept after registration.

The doll never says or shows a family name to a stranger. Guest and reviewer accounts cannot see registered faces or names.

You can delete a member at any time on this page. Deleting removes the face vectors and the member record.

By ticking the box, you confirm that the person in the photo agrees to this use.
